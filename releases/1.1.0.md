**Puls 1.1** – now in English and German, with a Liquid Glass app icon and a signed, notarized build.

- 🌍 **English & German** – Puls follows your system language
- 🧊 **New Liquid Glass app icon** made with Icon Composer (adapts to light, dark and tinted modes)
- ✅ **Signed with Developer ID and notarized by Apple** – no more security warning on first launch
- 🔔 **Update notice** – Puls checks GitHub once a day and shows new versions in the panel (can be turned off)
- ♿️ **Accessibility** – VoiceOver labels throughout, respects “Reduce motion”
- 🧪 Unit tests and CI on Apple silicon and Intel

**Install:** download `Puls-1.1.0.zip`, unzip, move `Puls.app` to Applications and open it. Requires macOS 26 (Tahoe).
