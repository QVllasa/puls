# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Everyday to ambitious Mac users on macOS 26 who want to know what their Mac is doing right now: why the fans spin up, what is eating memory, how fast the network is, how healthy the battery is. They arrive from the Mac App Store, GitHub, or a search for an iStat Menus alternative, usually on the Mac itself, curious but not willing to read a manual. The job of the site: understand in seconds what Puls shows and how it looks, trust it, and download it (Mac App Store or GitHub).

## Product Purpose
Puls is a free, open-source system monitor for the macOS menu bar with a Liquid Glass panel. It shows CPU, graphics, memory, network, disk, thermals and battery at a glance, in English and German. The website (qvllasa.github.io/puls, published by Vllasa Ventures UG) exists to present the app honestly, explain the difference between the GitHub and App Store versions, provide support, privacy and legal pages, and turn visitors into users. Success: visitors grasp the product from the first screen, see the real interface, and download it.

## Brand Personality
Calm, precise, premium. Like a well-made instrument: quiet, confident, exact, with care in every detail. The voice is plain and friendly, never salesy; it states what Puls does in concrete terms. Emotional goal: "this feels native to my Mac and made with care."

## Anti-references
- Generic SaaS landing pages: identical icon-card grids, gradient washes, hero-metric blocks, "Get started →" chrome.
- Emoji or text glyphs as feature icons.
- Loud gamer or hacker aesthetics (neon on black, terminal cosplay).
- Cluttered spec sheets that list every sensor.
References in the right lane: indie Mac app pages (Panic, Rogue Amoeba, Bjango/iStat), Apple product pages (large real product imagery, quiet type, generous space), Raycast and Linear (precision, restraint).

## Design Principles
1. Show the real thing: the actual panel and menu bar carry the page, not illustrations or claims.
2. Native, not generic: feel like it belongs on macOS Tahoe, borrowing its materials sparingly and purposefully.
3. Quiet confidence: one memorable moment, everything else disciplined.
4. Honest about trade-offs: the GitHub vs App Store differences are stated plainly.
5. Every word earns its place, in both languages.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.2 AA: text contrast at least 4.5:1, visible keyboard focus, full keyboard navigation, meaningful alt text for every screenshot, prefers-reduced-motion respected, no information conveyed by color alone, content readable at 200% zoom, proper lang attributes for English and German pages.
