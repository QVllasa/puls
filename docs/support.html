Support

Questions, problems or ideas about Puls? Email info@vllasa.com or open an issue on GitHub. We usually reply within two business days.

Where is Puls after launching it?

Puls is a menu bar app and does not appear in the Dock. Its item with your live values sits in the top-right of the menu bar. Click it to open the panel; right-click for a small menu.

How do I quit Puls?

Right-click the Puls item and choose “Quit Puls” – or open Settings with the gear button in the panel and click “Quit”.

How do I start Puls automatically?

Puls asks on first launch. You can change it any time in Settings under “Open at login”.

Which values can I show in the menu bar?

CPU, graphics, memory, network, disk and battery – each can be switched on or off, with colored status indicators or in plain monochrome.

Why doesn’t the App Store version show CPU temperatures and fans?

Apps from the Mac App Store run in a protected environment (the App Sandbox) that does not allow access to the Mac’s temperature sensors and fans. The App Store version shows your Mac’s thermal state, the power draw and the battery temperature instead. The free version on GitHub runs without the sandbox and additionally shows CPU temperatures, fan speeds and the top processes.

Can I have both versions installed?

Please use only one. Both are called “Puls”; if you switch, quit the old one and delete it from the Applications folder first.

Which languages are supported?

English and German. Puls follows the language set in System Settings → General → Language & Region.

System requirements

macOS 26 (Tahoe) or later, on Macs with Apple silicon or Intel.
